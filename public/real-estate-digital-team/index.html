Questions agents ask

Support for your office, with you still in charge.

Do I have to log into software every day to keep it working?

No daily task queue is required for routine follow-through. ARKON is set up to work in the background; you can use the workspace for detail and call your ARKON team when you want an update or help.

Does this replace my agents or staff?

No. It takes repeatable communication and coordination off their plate so they can focus on clients, negotiations, showings, and decisions.

Can it answer every real estate question?

No. It uses information and responses your office approves. Questions that need agent judgment, negotiation, legal advice, or a personal decision are handed off.

Is this just a chatbot on my website?

No. The website is one way people can reach your office. The digital back office can also support calls, follow-up, calendar coordination, inbox triage, records, and handoffs.
